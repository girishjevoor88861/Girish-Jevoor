Explainable AI Architecture

Genuine AI Intelligence Engine

No static mockups or fake labels. Every complaint undergoes real NLP classification, semantic similarity, and DBSCAN clustering.

🧠

Module 1: Automatic Classification

Analyzes free-form citizen text, extracts municipal keywords, detects urgency, and routes directly to the correct department.

Taxonomy: 7 Departments • 35 Subcategories
🔍

Module 2: Duplicate Detection

Cross-compares text similarity with Haversine GPS distance (< 350m) and temporal proximity to prevent duplicate dispatches.

Weights: 50% Text • 35% Geo • 15% Category
⚖️

Module 3: Explainable Priority

Transparent 0-100 score evaluating Safety (30%), Public Impact (25%), Urgency (20%), Concentration (15%), and Frequency (10%).

Immediate Hazard Life-Safety Overrides
📋

Module 4: Action Brief Generator

Produces structured, authority-ready operational briefs specifying exact severity, affected commuters, and recommended actions.

Commissioner-Ready Field Briefs
📍

Module 5: Geographic Hotspot Intelligence

Executes Haversine distance-based DBSCAN clustering to uncover recurring municipal problem zones, category concentrations, and unresolved bottlenecks.

Eps: 0.65km • Min Samples: 3 • Glowing Map Overlays
